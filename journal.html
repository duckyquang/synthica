Submission process

01

Pre-submission check

Verify originality, ethics compliance, formatting, and all authorship criteria before you submit.

02

Desk review

Editorial team evaluates scope, format, and completeness within 5 business days.

03

Double-blind peer review

Two independent reviewers evaluate methodology, clarity, references, and contribution. Target: 4 weeks.

04

Publication

Accepted articles published as HTML + PDF with DOI, CC BY 4.0, and full citation metadata.

Pre-submission checklist

  • 150–250 word structured abstract (Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion)
  • 3–8 semicolon-separated keywords
  • IEEE-style numbered references
  • Originality and authorship declaration signed
  • Ethics documentation for human subjects research
  • AI use disclosure where tools affected the manuscript

About the Journal

A rigorous, open record for student research

Synthica Journal is designed to function like a junior Nature or PLOS ONE — professional editorial standards, full metadata compliance, and zero paywalls. Student research deserves the same discoverability as published professional science.

01

Double-blind review

Manuscripts evaluated by at least two independent reviewers. Author and reviewer identities remain concealed throughout the entire process.

02

IEEE-aligned ethics

Publication ethics, COI disclosure, authorship standards, and AI use policy follow IEEE publication ethics and COPE guidelines.

03

Fully open access

Every article is immediately available under CC BY 4.0. No cost to authors or readers. No embargo. No article processing charges.

04

Scholar-ready pages

Article pages include citation metadata, stable DOI-linked URLs, crawlable abstracts, and downloadable PDFs for Google Scholar indexing.

Editorial Transparency

Policies built for trust

Our editorial policies follow IEEE publication ethics, COPE guidelines, and Society for Science safeguards. Full policy documents will be published with the journal launch.

✱

Peer Review

Double-blind, minimum two reviewers, conflict recusal, written rationale for every decision, and a 4-week target turnaround from reviewer assignment.

◎

Open Access

Immediate open access under CC BY 4.0 with no article processing charges for authors or reading fees for anyone.

◈

Authorship

All authors must meet ICMJE authorship criteria. CRediT taxonomy is used to document individual contributions transparently on every article.

❷

AI Disclosure

AI tools cannot be listed as authors. Substantive AI-generated content must be disclosed per IEEE AI ethics policy. Grammar tools do not require disclosure.

◇

Ethics & Integrity

No plagiarism, fabrication, falsification, or simultaneous submission. Human subjects research requires prior ethics approval documentation before review.

↻

Corrections

Factual errors corrected via published Erratum. Research fraud results in retraction with a clear, visible notice permanently attached to the article page.
