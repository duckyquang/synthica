About the journal

A professional record for student research

Synthica Journal exists to make student science citable, discoverable, and credible. The journal follows open-access publishing principles, double-blind peer review, and IEEE-aligned publication ethics while remaining readable for first-time authors.

01

Double-blind review

Manuscripts are evaluated by at least two independent reviewers whenever external peer review is required.

02

Ethics first

Publication ethics, conflicts of interest, authorship, AI use, and human-subjects safeguards are treated as launch requirements.

03

Scholar-ready pages

Article pages are planned around crawlable HTML, stable URLs, citation metadata, PDFs, and visible abstracts.

For authors

Submit research that reads like science, not a school assignment.

Original STEM research should use the IMRaD structure: Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion. Authors should be ready to disclose funding, conflicts of interest, ethics approval where applicable, data availability, author contributions, and substantive AI use.

Contact the Editorial Team

Pre-submission essentials

  • 150-250 word structured abstract
  • 3-8 keywords, separated by semicolons
  • IEEE-style numbered references
  • Originality and authorship confirmation
  • Ethics documentation for human subjects or regulated research
  • Clear AI disclosure when tools shaped the manuscript
