About the Journal

A rigorous, open record for student research.

Synthica is designed to function like a junior Nature or PLOS ONE — professional editorial standards, full metadata compliance, and zero paywalls. Student research deserves the same discoverability as published professional science.

We follow IEEE publication ethics, COPE guidelines, and Society for Science safeguards. Every article is published under CC BY 4.0 with a registered DOI, citation metadata, and indexed for Google Scholar discovery.

For Authors

Submission process

We accept original research (IMRaD), review articles, methods papers, perspectives, and commentaries. Authors must meet ethics, formatting, and disclosure requirements before peer review.

  1. 01

    Pre-submission check

    Verify originality, ethics compliance, formatting, and all authorship criteria before submitting.

  2. 02

    Desk review

    The Editorial Chair evaluates scope, format, and completeness within five business days.

  3. 03

    Double-blind peer review

    Two independent reviewers assess methodology, clarity, and contribution. Target: four weeks.

  4. 04

    Publication

    Accepted articles publish as HTML + PDF with DOI, CC BY 4.0, and full citation metadata.

Pre-submission checklist

  • 150–250 word structured abstract (Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion)
  • 3–8 semicolon-separated keywords
  • IEEE-style numbered references
  • Originality and authorship declaration signed by the corresponding author
  • Ethics documentation for any human-subjects research
  • AI use disclosure where tools materially affected the manuscript

Article types accepted

  • Original ResearchPrimary research in IMRaD format
  • Review ArticleSystematic or narrative reviews
  • Methods PaperNew methods or protocols with validation
  • PerspectiveInformed viewpoint on a research area
  • CommentaryResponse to a recent published article
  • Data ReportDataset description with quality controls

Editorial Policies

Policies built for trust

Our editorial policies follow IEEE publication ethics, COPE guidelines, and Society for Science safeguards. Full policy documents will be published with the journal launch.

  • Peer Review

    Double-blind, minimum two reviewers, conflict recusal, written rationale for every decision, four-week target turnaround.

    Read policy →

  • Open Access

    Immediate open access under CC BY 4.0. No article processing charges. Authors retain copyright.

    Read policy →

  • Authorship & CRediT

    All authors meet ICMJE criteria. CRediT taxonomy documents individual contributions transparently on every article.

    Read policy →

  • AI Disclosure

    AI tools cannot be authors. Substantive AI-generated content must be disclosed per IEEE policy. Grammar tools exempt.

    Read policy →

  • Publication Ethics

    No plagiarism, fabrication, falsification, or simultaneous submission. Human-subjects research requires prior ethics approval.

    Read policy →

  • Corrections & Retractions

    Factual errors corrected via published Erratum. Research fraud results in retraction with a permanent visible notice.

    Read policy →
